# Phase 2 — Strategy

## 1. Positioning Statement & Single Big Idea
- **Positioning Statement**: Konya Lider Nakliyat, 20 yıllık tecrübesi ve kendine ait asansörlü filosuyla, taşınma gününde sürpriz ek ücret çıkarmayan ve eşya güvenliğini yazılı taahhüt altına alan en şeffaf yerel ev taşıma ortağıdır.
- **The Single Big Idea**: *"Sürpriz Yok, Hasar Yok"* — Taşınma gününün tüm stresini ve belirsizliğini ortadan kaldıran, net fiyatlı ve asansör destekli sıfır-hata güvencesi.

## 2. Buyer Mental State on Arrival
- **What they just did**: Browsed 3 other generic, visual-soup nakliyat sites. Spoke to a friend who had a terrible moving experience (belongings broken, price doubled on the vehicle loading).
- **What they fear**: 
  - Price hiking after loading the truck ("hamaliye farkı", "bina asansörü bozuk", "eşya çokmuş").
  - Unprofessional day-labor crews who smoke inside the house, mishandle antique/expensive furniture, and speak rudely.
  - Delivery delays or cancellation at the last minute.
  - Items being held hostage or getting lost in transit.
- **What makes them close the tab**:
  - Stock images of generic blue trucks on highways or happy families unpacking in spotless high-end flats.
  - Lack of a direct phone/WhatsApp CTA or a complicated multi-step contact form that feels like a lead generator rather than a local operator.
  - Stiff, corporate copy full of "kaliteden ödün vermeden" that does not address their practical fears.

## 4. Sitemap & Revenue Rationale
- **Tier 1: Core Landing Pages**
  - **Ana Sayfa (Home)**: High conversion focus. Direct click-to-call/WhatsApp options. Establishes the brand, trust guarantees, and core services.
- **Tier 2: Key Service Pages (Revenue-focused SEO & GEO)**
  - **Asansörlü Evden Eve Nakliyat**: The highest-margin service. Crucial for Konya's multi-story apartment profile.
  - **Şehirlerarası Evden Eve Nakliyat**: High-ticket value page. Targets routes from Konya to major cities.
  - **Şehiriçi Evden Eve Nakliyat**: High-volume traffic driver. Focuses on local district moves.
- **Tier 3: Trust & Contact Pages**
  - **Firma Profili (Hakkımızda)**: Explains the 20-year history, real assets, and team profiles.
  - **İletişim / Teklif Al**: Dynamic quote builder designed to capture high-intent leads.
- **What We Cut & Why**:
  - *Cut: "Faaliyet Videoları" as a separate page*. YouTube embeds belong inline as proof assets on landing pages. A separate media page has zero search intent and leaks traffic.
  - *Cut: Separate pages for "Eşya Paketleme" and "Ekspertiz"*. These are features of a move, not standalone revenue services. They are integrated as value blocks on the main service pages.
  - *Cut: Over-segmented district pages (e.g., Sarayönü, Kulu)*. Very low search volume; creating them leads to thin duplicate content risks. We limit district pages to high-density targets (Selçuklu, Karatay, Meram, Ereğli).

## 5. Three Buyer Journeys

### Journey 1: The Urgent Mover (< 2 Weeks)
- **User State**: High stress. Already has a move-out date. Needs a fast quote and confirmed availability.
- **Scroll Path**: Hero Section (skips text) → Visual Slider/Gallery (checks vehicles/crew) → CTAs (Tap to Call / WhatsApp).
- **Friction Points**: Long forms, slow site loading, complex pricing calculation.
- **Exit Risk**: If they can't call or get a WhatsApp confirmation within 10 seconds.
- **Intervention**: Sticky mobile CTA bar containing direct "Telefonla Ara" and "WhatsApp Teklif Al" icons.

### Journey 2: The Strategic Planner (2–3 Months)
- **User State**: Calm, researching. Wants to compare quality, insurance terms, and logistics.
- **Scroll Path**: Hero → Services Grid → Trust Section (check insurance limits, guarantees) → FAQAccordion → Firma Profili.
- **Friction Points**: Hype, lack of detail about packing materials, vague insurance promises.
- **Exit Risk**: If the site looks like a template and fails to provide detailed operational specifications.
- **Intervention**: A detailed "Nasıl Taşıyoruz?" step-by-step trust strip showing real equipment (AVIF format) and explicit written guarantee copy.

### Journey 3: The Comparison Shopper (Tab Four)
- **User State**: Tabs open for 4 other firms. Comparing prices and checking for red flags.
- **Scroll Path**: Hero → Services Grid → Pricing factors table → Guarantee details.
- **Friction Points**: Same generic list of services as the other 4 tabs.
- **Exit Risk**: Zero differentiation in value proposition.
- **Intervention**: A bold "Neden Farklıyız?" comparison matrix highlighting written sabit-fiyat (fixed-price) guarantees and permanent crew vs. day laborers.

## 7. The 10 Most Damaging Moving Website Mistakes

1. **Invisible H1 / SEO Spams**: Using white-on-white text or single dots for spacing instead of rich semantic titles. Cost: Suppresses organic rankings.
2. **Hidden Brand / Domain Match Spam**: Using names like "Konya Evden Eve Nakliye" with zero mention of "Lider" on the actual logo. Cost: Destroys human trust.
3. **No Address or Tax Info**: Failing to list a local business license/address. Cost: Signals a "pirate" operation.
4. **Interactive Form Overload**: Requiring email, moving from/to full details, parcel counts on step one. Cost: 40%+ drop in mobile conversions.
5. **[Counterintuitive] Over-promising "100% Hasarsızlık Garantisi"**: Claims that "we never break anything" feel fake. Safe transport is a process, not magic. Cost: Triggers shopper skepticism.
6. **[Counterintuitive] FAQ-Stuffed Silent Objections**: Writing questions like "Crew stealing items?" or "Will you raise the price on the day?" in the FAQ. Cost: Plants fears the user didn't have.
7. **[Counterintuitive] Showing Empty Trucks on Highways**: Stock images of empty white cargo trucks look sterile. People want to see their household goods being padded and carried. Cost: Lowers conversion rates.
8. **Broken WhatsApp API links**: Links that open blank chats or require saving the contact first. Cost: Immediate conversion loss.
9. **No Mobile Safe Insets**: Sticky call bars overlapping the iOS home indicator or browser nav controls. Cost: Renders buttons unclickable on modern devices.
10. **Hidden Doorway Footers**: Links to 50 district names stuffed at the bottom of page profiles. Cost: Risk of Google Manual Actions for spam.
