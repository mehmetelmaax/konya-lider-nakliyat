# Phase 4 — Local SEO + GEO

## 4.1 RANKING REALITY

### SERP Anatomy & Mobile Click Distribution
Mobile search for *"konya evden eve nakliyat"* is heavily dominated by:
1. **Google Local Service Ads / Google Ads (LSA)**: Takes ~35% of clicks.
2. **Google Local Pack (Map Pack)**: Takes ~45% of clicks. This is the most crucial organic surface.
3. **Organic Search Results**: Takes ~20% of clicks. Most organic slots are occupied by directory aggregators (Armut, Evden Eve Nakliyat platformları, Sahibinden). A single company website can only realistically rank below these, meaning Map Pack visibility is the first priority.

### The Three Local Pack Factors the Website Cannot Influence
1. **User Proximity**: Google ranks listings based on how close the searcher is to the business's registered address at the time of query.
2. **Searcher’s Physical Location**: A query performed in Selçuklu returns Selçuklu listings; a searcher in Akşehir gets different results.
3. **GBP Age / Verification History**: Older listings have higher ranking baseline authority which cannot be coded.

### Timeline & Expectation Management
- **Month 1–3**: Zero direct rank guarantees. Focus is on establishing a clean GBP citation base and crawling baseline. Website structure is deployed.
- **Month 3–6**: Ranking improvements in specific targeted districts (Selçuklu, Meram) for low-competition terms.
- **Month 6–12**: Steady local visibility in Selçuklu Map Pack and organic rankings on page 1 of Google for high-volume keywords.

### The Current Bottleneck
The business lacks a verified Google Business Profile linked to the domain and has zero real customer reviews. This is the ultimate roadblock. No website optimizations will bypass this lack of Map Pack trust.

  - Correct: `/bolgeler/selcuklu-evden-eve-nakliyat`
  - Correct: `/bolgeler/meram-evden-eve-nakliyat` (not `çukurova`)

## 4.6 GEO-2: AI OVERVIEWS & CITATION OPTIMIZATION

### Retrieval / Grounding Ground Rules
LLMs extract answers from websites that present structured, factual data. An AI engine looks for direct semantic matches. To be citable:
- **No backward pronouns**: Never start paragraphs with *"Firmamız..."* or *"Biz..."*. Start with the brand name or entity.
- **The Grounding Rule**: The first sentence under any `H2` must be a self-contained factual statement containing a number or entity.

### Worked Examples (Query -> H2 -> First Sentence)

- **Query**: *"konya evden eve nakliyat fiyatları ne kadar"*
  - **H2**: Konya Evden Eve Nakliyat Fiyatları Nasıl Belirlenir?
  - **Extractable First Sentence**: Konya evden eve nakliyat fiyatları 2026 yılı itibarıyla taşınacak odaların sayısına, asansör ihtiyacına ve Selçuklu merkezli taşıma mesafesine göre 8.000 TL ile 22.000 TL arasında değişmektedir.

- **Query**: *"asansörlü nakliyat kaçıncı kata çıkar"*
  - **H2**: Asansörlü Ev Taşıma Hangi Katlara Ulaşır?
  - **Extractable First Sentence**: Konya Lider Nakliyat firmasının dış cephe mobil asansörleri maksimum 15. kat seviyesine kadar olan binalara eşya taşıma hizmeti vermektedir.

- **Query**: *"nakliyede marangoz hizmeti var mı"*
