## AUDITS & TESTS

### 1. AI-Tell & Jargon Audit
- *Quoted Failure*: *"müşteri memnuniyetini en üst seviyede tutmaktır"* (from the old profile page).
- *Why*: Classic machine-translated corporate fluff. Nobody says this out loud.
- *Rewrite*: *"Önceliğimiz eşyalarınızı yeni evinize hasarsız ulaştırmaktır."*

### 2. Redundancy Audit
- *Quoted Failure*: Sigorta and Güvenli mentioned 6 times in close proximity on the home services list.
- *Why*: Repetitive praise triggers suspicion.
- *Rewrite*: Grouped sigorta details exclusively under the Intercity Service page and the Trust Section, removing references from local city moves copy.

### 3. Specificity Audit
- *Quoted Failure*: *"Kaliteli ve ekonomik taşımacılık"*
- *Why*: Any cheap pirate carrier can put this on their window tomorrow.
- *Rewrite*: *"K3 belgeli filomuz ve kadrolu marangozumuzla sabit fiyatlı taşımacılık."*

### 4. Self-Tests
- **Spoken Test**: *"Taşınma günü ekstra iş veya hamaliye adı altında ek ücret talep etmiyoruz."* (Reads naturally, sounds like an Konya contractor speaking on the phone).
- **Swap Test**: Swap "nakliyat" with "tesisat". *"Selçuklu'da asansörlü tesisat aracımız..."* (Fails. The copy is highly specific to cargo box size, marangoz assembly, and high-rise balconies).
- **Plant Test**: We classified amateur crew fears as **SILENT**. We neutralized it by showcasing photos of uniformed staff, rather than saying *"Hırsız veya amatör hamal çalıştırmıyoruz"*, which would plant the fear.
- **Concession Test**: The pricing FAQ concedes: *"Fiyat aralığımız kat yüksekliğine ve mesafeye göre değişir."* (Honest operational truth builds credibility).
